How to split utility bills with flatmates fairly

5 min read

Rent is easy to split; bills are where flatshares argue. Who uses more electricity? What if someone was away for a month? A few simple rules prevent most of it.

Split equally by default

For internet, water and most utilities, equal shares are fair enough and avoid endless debates. Agree on it when someone moves in.

When to use a different split

  • Bigger room or private bathroom: a larger share of rent, sometimes of bills too.
  • Someone away for weeks: agree in advance whether they still pay the full share.
  • Partner who practically lives there: add them as an extra share.

Someone moving out mid-cycle

Split the bill by days: if the bill covers 60 days and they lived there 30, they pay half of their usual share. Settle it before they leave, not when the bill arrives two months later.

FAQ

How do flatmates split utility bills?

Most flatshares split utilities equally and adjust only for clear differences, like a bigger room or someone living there part-time.

How do you split a bill when a flatmate moves out?

Pro-rate by days: divide the bill by the days it covers and charge each person for the days they lived there.
